Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 5, 2025

Zunix is built by students, for students — so we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your data as part of powering Africa’s first youth-led tech ecosystem.

🔍 What We Collect
  • Personal Info: name, email address, phone number (if provided)
  • Academic Info: school, level, field of study
  • Device Info: browser, OS, device model, IP address
  • Platform Activity: pages visited, time spent, points earned
  • Engagement Score: your Zunix interaction level
  • Content Data: posts, uploads, bookings, reviews
  • Event Data: RSVPs, attendance logs, mission completions
🧠 Why We Collect It
  • Track your growth, earnings, and rewards
  • Match you to relevant programs or marketplace gigs
  • Showcase your talents (opt-in only)
  • Improve features based on usage data
  • Prevent spam, fraud, or misuse
🤝 Who We Share It With
  • We never sell your data.
  • We may share anonymized stats for grants/investors
  • We only share personal data when you opt in
  • Legal requests may force disclosure in rare cases
🔐 Your Rights & Control
  • Download your data anytime
  • Edit or update your profile details
  • Request account deletion with full wipe
  • Control visibility settings and opt-ins

Contact: privacy@zunix.africa

🍪 Cookies & Tracking Tech
  • Session Cookies: keep you logged in
  • Analytics: understand how features are used
  • Referral Tracking: track user invites and bonuses

No ad trackers. You can manage preferences in your browser or visit our Cookie Policy.

📦 Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure cloud servers with full encryption and role-based access control. Payments go through secure third-party gateways (we don’t store card info).

⏳ Data Retention

We keep your data as long as your account is active. If inactive for over 12 months, data may be archived. You can request deletion at any time.

🧾 Legal Basis & Global Protection

Though we’re Students-first, we comply with major standards like the GDPR. We believe every student deserves world-class digital rights protection.

📩 Policy Updates

We’ll notify you before any major change via email or in-app prompt. Stay tuned as Zunix evolves!

💬 Student FAQ

Can I use Zunix anonymously?
You can limit how much info shows publicly, but a valid email is always required.

Will brands see my data?
Only if you join a brand campaign or giveaway. We always ask first.
